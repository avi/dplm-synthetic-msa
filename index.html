DPLM synthetic MSAs: ten-protein OpenFold-3 benchmark

Release candidate v0.1.0 · 6 October 2026

Observed result: 50 unconstrained DPLM variants at 40% masking improve mean TM-score over no MSA in 9 of 10 proteins. Same query, OpenFold checkpoint, model seed and sample count across conditions. This is a measured input effect; evolutionary covariation, variant function and generalization to unseen proteins are not established by this experiment.

305 predictions are included once: 275 in the main comparison, 15 in the 250-variant depth extension, and 15 from superseded constrained trials. Each condition has five diffusion samples from one model seed. Query-only controls are available for the seven newer proteins. All structures, confidence files, MSAs, accepted variant pools and generation audits are included.

What changes with masking?

Higher masking is protein-dependent. In the shallow antifreeze case, query-only TM-score is 0.243, 40% synthetic masking reaches 0.726, and 60% masking falls to 0.198; the natural MSA reaches 0.957. Myoglobin, CI2 and acylphosphatase approach natural-MSA accuracy with synthetic input. Ubiquitin and Top7 already fold well without additional sequences.

Interpretation and protocol

  • Main generation: original Gumbel sampler; no temperature increase, permanently protected residues, identity filtering, adaptive masking or fold-score selection. First 50 unique canonical query-length variants; query copies excluded. Unmasked random positions supply query context per candidate.
  • OpenFold-3 0.4.0, checkpoint of3-p2-155k; model seed 2746317213, five samples, no templates or ligands. Every arm predicts the same query sequence; generated variants are MSA rows.
  • No-MSA flags produce a gap row and zero profile in this version. Query-only preserves query-derived MSA features. These are distinct baselines; no neural-network layers are removed.
  • Ten short, structurally characterized proteins constitute a convenience sample. Model training overlap is possible; five correlated samples are not independent replicate experiments. No random-mutation control or experimental functional validation was performed.
  • RMSD is whole-chain Cα Kabsch RMSD, TM-score uses TM-align normalized by reference length, and lDDT here uses Cα distances. Confidence is a model output, not experimental accuracy.

Reference details

  • dhfr: Reference has N37D relative to query and folate/NADP ligands omitted in prediction
  • thioredoxin: Oxidized thioredoxin; crystallization copper and MPD omitted from predictions
  • cyclophilin: Cyclosporin A-bound reference; cyclosporin omitted from predictions
  • ubiquitin: Chain A, all observed contiguous residues; no templates or ligands provided.
  • myoglobin: Chain A, all observed contiguous residues; no templates or ligands provided. Heme omitted from predictions.
  • lysozyme: Chain A, all observed contiguous residues; no templates or ligands provided.
  • ci2: Chain A, all observed contiguous residues; no templates or ligands provided. Labels 19–83; unresolved N terminus excluded.
  • top7: Chain A, all observed contiguous residues; no templates or ligands provided. Designed negative control; labels 3–94; MSE37 mapped to canonical methionine; terminal tag excluded.
  • acylphosphatase: Chain A, all observed contiguous residues; no templates or ligands provided. NMR first model reference.
  • antifreeze: Ocean pout type III antifreeze protein; observed contiguous chain A. No templates or ligands. Uses deposited recombinant sequence, labels 1–66, omits unresolved C-terminal KDEL; PDB marks one mutation.
