Upstream software, models and experimental data
===============================================

DPLM: https://github.com/bytedance/dplm
Source commit: 8a2e15e53416b4536f03f79ad1f6f6a9cbd5e19d
Source license: Apache-2.0; copy in third_party/DPLM-LICENSE.txt.
Checkpoint: https://huggingface.co/airkingbd/dplm_650m
Revision: 7a7e651baa667d094aba05e9dc1cf52a3332110a
The wrapper fetches upstream inference code and weights at runtime; neither is
vendored into this release. Model weights retain their upstream terms.

OpenFold-3: https://github.com/aqlaboratory/openfold-3
Version 0.4.0; source af111e167d3035fb10a048d2c1608243b660b443.
Source license: Apache-2.0; copy in third_party/OpenFold3-LICENSE.txt.
Checkpoint: of3-p2-155k. The wrapper downloads it to your Modal account.
Source and model/data terms remain those of their respective upstream releases.

Foldism: https://github.com/hgbrian/foldism
The OpenFold Modal wrapper credits Foldism's backends/openfold3.py as its basis.
MIT notice preserved in third_party/foldism-LICENSE.txt.
The wrapper adds custom MSA packaging, configuration, caching and local output
handling; it is not the unmodified upstream Foldism source.

Boltz: https://github.com/jwohlwend/boltz
The optional MSA-precompute helper imports Boltz's MMseqs2 client.
MIT notice in third_party/boltz-LICENSE.txt. Boltz code is not vendored here.
Natural alignments were retrieved through https://api.colabfold.com using the
Boltz client. Database snapshots and returned search hits may change over time;
exact benchmark input MSAs are included rather than relying on repeat searches.

labeled separately and are not experimental structures. Original query sources
for the first three proteins: UniProt P0ABQ4, P0AA25 (initiator Met removed),
P62937. Remaining queries use the reference's resolved sequence as documented.

NumPy, Biopython, TM-tools/TM-align, Matplotlib, Requests and Modal are external
dependencies. Their own notices and terms apply; packages are not vendored.
